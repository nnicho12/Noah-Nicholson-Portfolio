## Mistakes Made

I believe I messed up with the extrusions for both parts of the mount. I extruded both to 15 mm with both overlapping the other. This means that the mount has extra thickness to it and it may produce results that vary from what I calculated.

## Actual Time

This assignment took around 2 hours to complete due to having to restart my calculations multiple times.

## Lessons learned

I learned to first make sure the structure that I want to create is the final and most engineered it can be so I can avoid having to make large changes to it after calculations therefore, invalidating those calculations and needing to start over again. This will reduce workload and create more thoughtful models.
