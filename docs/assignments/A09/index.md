## Lessons Learned

During this process I learned to try and visualize my work and what the math meant in terms of the pulley and frame. It helped me make more sense of what the equations meant if I could see how forces interact and so on. I also had to choose a different material for the cad model because Solidworks did not have the exact material I intended to use. I used an alternative based on the properties it had.
